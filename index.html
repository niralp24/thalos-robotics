01 · About Thalos

Built by students.
Built to last.

A new team with the structure of a serious engineering program.

Thalos Robotics is independent and open to high school students across Silicon Valley. Members take meaningful ownership of design decisions, prototypes, testing, documentation, and competition preparation.

As a founding member, students will help establish the team’s technical standards, culture, workflow, and long-term direction—not simply join a project that is already finished.

01

Student ownership

Members contribute to real decisions, build work, testing, and presentations.

02

Systems thinking

Mechanical, electrical, software, and operations work as one integrated team.

03

Iteration first

Concepts become prototypes, prototypes enter the water, and evidence drives revisions.

02 · The program

More than building
a robot.

The MATE ROV Competition combines engineering, underwater mission performance, safety, technical writing, and professional presentation.

The challenge

Design for real underwater missions.

Teams develop remotely operated vehicles and supporting systems to complete tasks inspired by ocean science, environmental monitoring, and marine infrastructure.

03 · Subteams

Different skills.
One system.

Students do not need prior underwater robotics experience. Curiosity, consistency, and a willingness to learn matter most.

MECH / 01

Mechanical

CAD, frame design, buoyancy, manipulators, fabrication, tooling, and physical testing.

  • 3D design and prototyping
  • Mission mechanisms
  • Waterproof structures
ELEC / 02

Electrical

Power distribution, wiring, controls hardware, cameras, sensors, connectors, and safety.

  • Power architecture
  • Sensor integration
  • System reliability
SOFT / 03

Software

Operator controls, camera interfaces, telemetry, testing tools, and automated systems.

  • Vehicle control
  • Operator interface
  • Data and automation
OPS / 04

Operations

Piloting, tether management, documentation, presentations, media, logistics, and strategy.

  • Mission execution
  • Technical communication
  • Team organization
