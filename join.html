Founding members will help shape the team’s systems, culture, and first competition season.

Who should apply

There is more than one way to build a robot.

No single background required. We need people across every discipline: from hands-on builders to programmers to writers. Prior robotics experience is preferred but a foundation in electronics or engineering is enough to get started.

Strong founding members are curious, dependable, willing to learn, and able to work respectfully with people whose skills are different from their own.

Frame design, gripper arm, buoyancy tuning, and float construction.

  • CAD and prototyping
  • Fabrication and assembly
  • Mechanism testing
ELEC / 02

Electrical

Wiring, power systems, ESC setup, waterproof penetrators, and safety inspection.

  • Power distribution
  • Connectors and cameras
  • Electrical safety
SOFT / 03

Software

ROV control systems, gamepad input, camera feed, and float depth logic.

  • Controls and interfaces
  • Sensor data
  • Testing tools
DOCS / 04

Documentation

Engineering notebook, technical report, presentation prep, and marketing display.

  • Technical writing
  • Design evidence
  • Presentation visuals
OPS / 05

Pilot

ROV pilot and co-pilot: drives the vehicle and manages the tether during competition.

  • Mission strategy
  • Communication
  • Repetition under pressure

Application process

Four simple steps.

  1. 01
    Submit the interest form

    Tell us about your interests, experience, and what you want to learn.

  2. 02
    Meet the team

    Learn more about Thalos, ask questions, and discuss where you may contribute.

  3. 03
    Complete onboarding

    Review expectations, communication, safety, and the current season plan.

  4. 04
    Start contributing

    Join a task, learn the tools, and begin documenting your work.
